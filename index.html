Co-founded by Maxwell Frank and Tye French, Ridgeline Productions is a full-service production company based in Santa Barbara, California.

With over a decade of experience traveling the globe, we produce, direct, and create authentic brand campaigns and commercial films for startups, major corporations, and agencies alike.
